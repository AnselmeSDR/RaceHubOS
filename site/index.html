    Racing in three steps

    1. Download the installer for your computer.

      Download for Windows Download for macOS

    2. Double-click it. It installs what is missing (Git, Node.js), then RaceHubOS, and puts a shortcut on your desktop. On macOS, open the zip, then right-click the file and choose Open the first time.

    3. Open RaceHubOS: it shows up in your browser. In Settings, search for your track and connect the Control Unit.

    You need a Carrera Digital 132 or 124 track with its Control Unit 30352 and the AppConnect 30369, and a computer running Windows, macOS or Linux.

    Updates come from within the app: Settings, Check, then Update.

    Questions before you start

    Does my computer need Bluetooth?

    Yes: RaceHubOS talks to the AppConnect 30369 over Bluetooth Low Energy, from the computer. Most laptops have it built in.

    Does it work with the Digital 124?

    Yes. The Digital 124 uses the same Control Unit 30352 and the same AppConnect 30369 as the 132.

    How many cars can race?

    Up to six at once, one per controller: as many as the Control Unit handles.

    Can I try it without a track?

    Yes: the built-in simulator runs virtual cars, with no Control Unit.

    Which browser do I need?

    Any recent browser. The Bluetooth link goes through the computer, not the browser.

    Do I need internet?

    Only to install it, update it or add add-ons. Races run without internet: everything stays on your computer.

    Is it really free?

    Yes: no account, no subscription, no ads. RaceHubOS is open source, under AGPL-3.0.

    Free, open, made by racers

    Carrera dropped its official app, and the alternatives are closed. RaceHubOS is licensed under AGPL-3.0: free to use, change and share, and every modified version stays open.
